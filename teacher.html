ℹ️ What do the activity signals mean?

The 📊 Activity panel inside each student card summarizes their behavior on the site. Treat these as patterns and signals, not as proof of cheating — client-side logging can be spoofed.

login
The student logged in. Marks the start of a session.
logout
The student clicked Log out. Duration = how long they were logged in.
logout-auto
The site logged them out automatically after 20 minutes of inactivity.
unload
The browser tab was closed while they were still logged in.
heartbeat
Sent every 60 seconds the tab is visible. The count powers the "~X min active" number in the summary.
blur
The student switched to another tab or window. "Tab-aways" counts these.
focus
The student returned to this tab. Duration = how long they were away.
paste
The student pasted text into a note box. Detail shows length plus the first 80 characters of what was pasted.
note-save
A note was saved. Detail shows note length, pastes during this note, and time on the page; Duration = time since their first keystroke.
video-click
The student clicked one of the Watch & learn video links. Detail shows the video name and URL.

A red 🚩 flag next to a student name (and a red edge on the card) means the activity matched one or more of these signals — visible without expanding anything:
• any paste into a note box,
• 5+ tab-aways during the session, or
• a note saved with under 5 seconds of typing since the first keystroke (implausibly fast).
Hover the 🚩 to see exactly which signals fired. The Time per page line shows how many minutes the student spent on each organ (each heartbeat ≈ 1 minute of active viewing), so you can spot off-task browsing even when no notes were saved. Treat these as signals, not proof.
